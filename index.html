About

Gather, Grind, Grow

Gather — coffee is how we welcome people in. A table, a quiet morning, a friend who drops by.

Grind — the small daily work: whole bean, fresh roast, a cup made with care.

Grow — community that deepens over time. Faith is our quiet why — gathering and growth, not a sermon on the bag. Just honest coffee meant to be shared.
