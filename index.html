How does shipping work?

After you order, we roast and ship to your address. Timing and carriers will be confirmed in Shopify (expect a short roast window, then transit). Demo only — final rates and zones set at launch.

Why whole bean?

Whole bean stays fresher longer. Grind just before brewing for the brightest cup. Ground options can be added later in Shopify if you want them.

How fresh is the coffee?

We roast in small batches after you order, then ship so the bag arrives still lively. Exact roast-to-ship timing will be published with the live store.
